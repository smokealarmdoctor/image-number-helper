# Photographers' forums (week 5): a tip, not an ad

Threads where people ask for numbered thumbnails for client proofing (from the research, all
unverified here): dgrin "Proofing numbers on thumbnails", WordPress NextGEN "order client
numbered photos", Adobe Lightroom community "numbering images". **Vendor support forums
(Adobe, WordPress.org) usually do not welcome third-party plugs.** Prefer a general photographers'
forum or a new thread of your own, and if a thread is years old, start a new one instead of
replying.

Be upfront about the limit: the numbers appear on **your** screen. The client only sees them if
you share your screen or send them a numbered screenshot (the screenshot is a Pro feature).

**Title:** Tip: numbering the photos on a gallery page so clients can say "I like 4, 9 and 17"

**Body:**

If you send clients an online gallery and get feedback like "the one with the red door," here's a
workaround I use. A small Chrome extension I wrote, Image Number Helper, puts a visible number on
every image on whatever page you have open. On a video call you share your screen and say "number 9."
Or take a numbered screenshot and email it so they can reply with numbers.

Honest limits: it only labels what's on your screen (not the client's), and it's not a proofing
system, so selections still live in your email or chat. Free for the first 8 images per page, Pro
is $2.99 once and removes the limit and adds the numbered screenshot. I'm the developer.
