Refund policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

30-day money-back guarantee. If Image Number Helper Pro isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of purchase and you’ll get the full amount back. No questions asked.

How to request a refund

Open a request on our support page, or use the link in your Stripe receipt email to contact the seller. Tell us the email you used at checkout. Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

After a refund

Your Pro license is cancelled and should no longer be used. The free version of the extension keeps working. Licenses are checked offline on your device, so Pro features may keep working until you remove the license (extension popup → Upgrade → Remove license) or update to a version that blocks refunded keys.

Statutory rights

This policy doesn’t limit any rights you have under consumer protection law where you live, including any statutory withdrawal period for digital content.
